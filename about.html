About Us

Our Heritage

138 years of
unbroken precision

Founded in the Jura mountains of Switzerland in 1886 by Henri Stackly, our maison began with a single obsession: to create mechanisms that would outlive their owners.

Three generations and two world wars later, that obsession remains unchanged. Every watchmaker we hire apprentices for six years before touching a calibre.

Our Values

Built on principles

01

Precision

Every component is machined to tolerances of ±0.003mm. We use Swiss Made certified movements and reject 40% of parts during quality control.

02

Longevity

Our timepieces are designed to last centuries with proper care. We stock spare parts for every model we have ever produced since 1910.

03

Heritage

We refuse to compromise tradition for trend. Each new calibre must pass the spirit of Henri Stackly's original design philosophy.

The Calibre
